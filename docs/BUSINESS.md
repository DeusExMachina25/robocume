# Making money with Robocume

Goal: real income from a low starting cost, sellable anywhere. Ordered from
cheapest and fastest to most expensive. Numbers are rough guesses to test, not facts.

## 1. Build in public (cost: ₹0, start now)
Post every milestone on YouTube, Instagram reels, X and Reddit (r/robotics, r/ROS,
r/homeassistant, r/3Dprinting). The scout leaving the mothership to go under a bed
is a very visual, shareable clip. This builds the audience every option below needs.

## 2. Sponsorships and donations (cost: ₹0)
GitHub Sponsors, Patreon or Buy Me a Coffee for early access to build logs and
CAD files. Small, but it pays for parts.

## 3. Paid build guide or course (cost: your time)
"Build a modular ROS2 robot vacuum from scratch". Sell globally on Gumroad or
Teachable, in USD. The code stays open; you sell the teaching, the videos and the
support. India-specific pricing for students.

## 4. Kits (cost: small batches of 10)
- Scout kit first: it's cheap (~₹6–7k in parts) and makes a fun standalone
  learning robot.
- PCBs from JLCPCB or PCBWay, parts bundled by you.
- Sell on Tindie (worldwide, low fees, made for open hardware) and in India
  through a simple store (Instamojo or Razorpay payment pages).

## 5. College workshops and lab kits (India)
Engineering colleges and robotics clubs pay for hands-on ROS2 workshops.
Robocume plus the simulation means students can learn without hardware first.

## 6. Portfolio → jobs abroad
A well-documented, working two-robot system with ROS2, embedded firmware and
hardware design is a strong portfolio for robotics roles in the EU, US, Japan
and the Gulf. Keep the repo clean; recruiters read it.

## 7. Later: assembled units and crowdfunding
Once kits sell, run a pre-order on Crowd Supply (made for open hardware) or
Kickstarter. Only take pre-orders once the design is proven.

## Protecting the brand cheaply
- The code and designs are open, so the **name** is the moat. Register the
  "Robocume" trademark in India (e-filing for individuals is about ₹4,500 per class).
- Others may copy the design; you win on being the original, the support, the
  community and quality kits.
